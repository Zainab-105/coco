COCO — Complete E-Commerce Website Requirements
Project: COCO Online Store
Purpose: Build a premium, mobile-first e-commerce website with a powerful admin panel to manage products, orders, customers, sales and business growth.
The website must have two connected parts:
   1. Customer-Facing Website — where customers browse products and place orders.
   2. Admin Panel — where I manage the entire store, customer database, orders, inventory, analytics and business operations.
1. Brand & Product Categories
   * Brand name: COCO
   * Premium, modern, luxury-inspired design.
   * Use COCO's approved brand identity and colour palette.
   * Categories:
   * Car Scents: Hanging Car Diffusers
   * Car Accessories: Car Ashtrays, Car Front Showpieces
   * Home Scents: Room Diffusers
   * Gift Sets
   * The website must allow new categories, products and variants to be added easily in the future.
Part A — Customer-Facing Website
2. Website Design & Navigation
   * Fully responsive design for mobile, tablet and desktop.
   * Mobile-first layout with easy, thumb-friendly navigation.
   * Premium, clean and professional interface.
   * Header with logo, menu, search, account and cart icons.
   * Easy navigation between all product categories.
   * Sticky or easily accessible mobile navigation.
   * Clear footer with contact details, policies and social media links.
   * Fast loading, smooth interactions and optimized images/videos.
   * Readable fonts, accessible colours and clearly visible buttons.
3. Product Categories & Listing Pages
   * Display products in a clean grid: preferably two products per row on mobile and more columns on desktop.
   * Every product card should show:
   * Product image
   * Product name
   * Price and sale price, if applicable
   * Discount, if available
   * Stock availability
   * Swipeable product images and videos: Customers should be able to swipe left or right on a product card to view its additional photos and videos without opening the product page.
   * Add clear dots or indicators to show that more media is available.
   * On desktop, provide arrows or another easy way to browse product media.
   * Quick View popup to see product details without leaving the category page.
   * Quick Add to Cart button.
   * Wishlist or favourite products feature.
   * Search bar with product suggestions.
   * Sorting by price, newest arrivals and popularity.
   * Filters for price, product type, fragrance and availability where relevant.
   * Load More or pagination for larger product collections.
4. Product Detail Pages
   * Multiple high-quality images from different angles.
   * Product videos displayed within the same gallery.
   * Full-screen image viewing and zoom functionality.
   * Product name, price, discount and detailed description.
   * Product features and specifications.
   * Fragrance names, scent profiles and notes for diffusers.
   * Product variations and fragrance selection where applicable.
   * Quantity selector and Add to Cart button.
   * Stock availability and delivery information.
   * Customer ratings and written reviews.
   * Customer-uploaded product photos and videos, subject to moderation.
   * Related products and gift-set recommendations.
   * Clearly displayed shipping, exchange and return policies.
5. Shopping Cart & Checkout
   * Mini-cart or slide-out cart showing products, quantities and total price.
   * Customers can change quantities or remove products easily.
   * Add to Cart without unnecessary page reloads.
   * Guest checkout without forcing customers to create an account.
   * Simple checkout with minimal form fields.
   * Collect customer name, phone number, email if needed, city and complete delivery address.
   * Clearly show product prices, discounts, delivery charges and final payable amount.
   * Support Cash on Delivery (COD).
   * Support JazzCash and Easypaisa advance payments where suitable integrations are available.
   * Payment status must be verified appropriately; uploading a payment screenshot alone must not automatically confirm payment.
   * Order confirmation page with order number and order summary.
   * Confirmation notifications through available email, SMS or WhatsApp integrations.
   * WhatsApp assistance for customers who need help placing an order.
6. Customer Accounts & Support
   * Optional customer accounts with order history and saved addresses.
   * Guest checkout must remain available.
   * Wishlist and recently viewed products.
   * WhatsApp support button across the website.
   * Contact page and frequently asked questions.
   * Clear shipping, returns, exchanges and refund policies.
   * Newsletter signup with customer consent and unsubscribe functionality.
   * Customer feedback and review submission.
   * Photo/video feedback uploads where supported.
   * Voice-note feedback where technically supported.
   * Customer feedback should be moderated before appearing publicly.
7. Gift Sets & Promotions
   * Dedicated Gift Sets category.
   * Product bundles with clear pricing.
   * Optional gift packaging or gift notes if offered.
   * Discount codes and promotional campaigns.
   * Seasonal sale banners and special offers.
   * Display original and discounted prices clearly.
   * Related product suggestions to encourage additional purchases.
Part B — COCO Owner Admin Panel
8. Admin Panel Overview
The admin panel must be a complete business management dashboard, not just a page for viewing orders.
   * Separate, secure owner login.
   * Easy-to-use interface that does not require coding for routine tasks.
   * Connected to the same database as the customer-facing website.
   * Automatically save website orders, customer records and product information.
   * Show business performance through summary cards, graphs and reports.
   * Allow me to manage the store independently.
   * Keep historical records so I can analyze business performance over months and years.
9. Main Dashboard & Business Insights
The dashboard should display important business information at a glance.
   * Total sales and revenue.
   * Total orders and new orders.
   * Pending, processing, shipped and completed orders.
   * Total customers and repeat customers.
   * Total products and low-stock products.
   * Cancelled orders, returns and refunds.
   * Best-selling products and categories.
   * Average order value.
   * Sales growth compared with previous periods.
   * Date filters for today, last 7 days, last 30 days, custom date range and yearly reports.
   * Interactive graphs that update as new data is saved.
   * Clear distinction between actual figures and estimated figures.
10. Order Management
Every order placed through the website must automatically be saved in the admin panel.
   * Unique order ID and order date.
   * Customer name, phone number and delivery address.
   * Products ordered, quantities, variations and prices.
   * Discounts, delivery charges and total order value.
   * Payment method and payment status.
   * Search orders by order ID, customer, date or phone number.
   * Filter orders by status, payment method and order value.
   * Update order statuses:
   * New
   * Confirmed
   * Processing
   * Shipped
   * Delivered
   * Cancelled
   * Returned
   * Refunded
   * Add internal notes to orders.
   * Record courier or tracking details where supported.
   * Print or download invoices and packing slips.
   * Export order records to CSV or Excel.
   * Maintain records of cancellations, refunds and returns.
11. Customer Database & CRM
The system must build a customer database automatically as orders are placed.
   * Save customer name, phone number, email if supplied and delivery addresses.
   * Link all orders to the relevant customer profile.
   * View each customer's complete order history.
   * Show total orders, total spending and average order value.
   * Record first purchase and most recent purchase dates.
   * Identify new customers, repeat customers, high-value customers and inactive customers.
   * Search and filter customers by name, phone number, email or purchase history.
   * Reduce duplicate customer profiles where possible.
   * Add internal customer notes where needed.
   * Export customer records for permitted business reporting and marketing.
   * Store marketing consent and opt-out status.
   * Protect customer information and restrict access to authorized users.
12. Product & Inventory Management
I must be able to manage products without coding.
   * Add, edit, duplicate and remove products.
   * Create and manage categories.
   * Update product names, descriptions, prices and discounts.
   * Upload multiple images and videos for each product.
   * Select featured product images.
   * Manage fragrance variants and product options.
   * Create and update Gift Sets.
   * Assign stock quantities and track availability.
   * Automatically update stock according to the configured order workflow.
   * Low-stock alerts and out-of-stock indicators.
   * Record product costs to support profit calculations.
   * Maintain inventory adjustment records.
   * Bulk import and export product information where supported.
13. Sales Analytics & Reporting
The admin panel must help me understand which products sell well and how the business is growing.
   * Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly sales graphs.
   * Revenue and order trends over time.
   * Sales by product and category.
   * Sales by fragrance or product variation.
   * Best-selling and slow-moving products.
   * Average order value and repeat-purchase rate.
   * COD versus advance-payment orders.
   * Cancellation, return and delivery-success rates where data is available.
   * Customer purchase trends and inactive customer reports.
   * Discount and promotion performance.
   * Low-stock and inventory reports.
   * Export reports to CSV or Excel.
Financial reporting:
   * Show gross sales, discounts, refunds and net sales separately.
   * Calculate estimated profit using product costs and any additional expenses entered.
   * Clearly distinguish revenue from profit.
   * Allow comparison between different dates and reporting periods.
14. Marketing & Customer Retention
   * Create and manage discount codes.
   * Manage sale banners and promotions.
   * Track coupon usage.
   * Manage newsletter subscribers.
   * Segment customers based on purchase history and spending.
   * Track abandoned carts where supported.
   * Support abandoned-cart recovery through suitable integrations.
   * Integrate Google Analytics and Google Search Console.
   * Track website traffic, product views and conversions where configured.
   * Provide useful insights to help decide which products to restock or promote.
   * Only send marketing messages when appropriate consent has been obtained.
15. Reviews, Feedback & Support Management
   * View and manage customer reviews.
   * Approve, hide or moderate reviews before publication.
   * View customer-uploaded photos and videos.
   * Manage voice-note feedback if implemented.
   * Track customer complaints and support requests.
   * Add internal notes and track resolution status.
   * Prevent spam and inappropriate uploads.
16. Admin Security & Data Protection
   * Secure owner login with strong authentication.
   * Two-factor authentication where supported.
   * Optional staff accounts with restricted permissions in the future.
   * Protect sensitive customer and financial information.
   * SSL certificate and secure website configuration.
   * Regular software and plugin updates.
   * Activity logs for important admin actions.
   * Automated backups and a documented recovery process.
   * Securely export orders, products, customers and reports.
   * Test the backup restoration process.
   * Ensure I retain ownership and control of the domain, hosting, website data and payment accounts.
Part C — Technical Requirements & Future Growth
17. SEO & Performance
   * SEO-friendly product and category pages.
   * Search-engine-friendly URLs, page titles and meta descriptions.
   * Product structured data where appropriate.
   * XML sitemap and Google Search Console setup.
   * Optimized images and efficient video loading.
   * Fast performance on mobile internet.
   * Responsive testing on mobile, tablet and desktop.
   * Accessible typography, readable text and clear interactive controls.
18. Platform, Budget & Maintenance
   * Prefer a reliable, easy-to-manage platform.
   * Use free or low-cost tools where practical.
   * If using WordPress and WooCommerce, identify which features are built in and which require additional plugins or custom development.
   * Disclose all paid plugins, hosting costs, payment gateway charges and recurring fees before development.
   * Avoid unnecessary plugins that affect performance or security.
   * Make routine updates possible without coding.
   * Keep the system expandable for more products, categories, traffic, staff and payment methods.
   * Provide a complete handover guide explaining how to manage products, process orders, update stock, export data and restore backups.
19. Final Testing & Acceptance Checklist
Before the website is considered complete, verify that:
   * Customers can browse all categories on mobile and desktop.
   * Customers can swipe through product photos and videos directly on product cards.
   * Quick View and Quick Add to Cart work correctly.
   * Checkout works and order details are saved accurately.
   * The owner can find and update orders in the admin panel.
   * Customer profiles and order histories are saved and searchable.
   * Products, photos, videos, prices and stock can be updated through the admin panel.
   * Dashboard graphs match the actual saved data for the selected date range.
   * Sales and profit figures are calculated and labelled correctly.
   * Reports can be exported.
   * Backups and recovery procedures are tested.
   * All paid tools, technical limitations and ongoing costs are disclosed.
   * The website is tested on real mobile screens and common desktop browsers.
20. Expected Final Deliverables
The developer must provide:
   * Complete customer-facing COCO e-commerce website.
   * Secure owner admin panel.
   * Configured product categories and checkout workflow.
   * Product-card photo/video swipe functionality.
   * Order management and customer database.
   * Product and inventory management.
   * Dashboard graphs and agreed business reports.
   * Payment integrations as agreed.
   * SEO and analytics setup.
   * Security, backup and data-export setup.
   * Admin access and ownership handover.
   * Written instructions for managing the website.
   * A list of included features, paid plugins, limitations and ongoing costs.
Final Goal
COCO needs a premium customer shopping experience and a powerful owner-controlled business dashboard. Customers should be able to discover products, preview media, add items to their cart and place orders easily. Meanwhile, I should be able to manage the entire business from one admin panel, with all order and customer records saved reliably and useful graphs and insights generated as the store grows.
Important: Advanced features such as custom analytics, profit reporting, abandoned-cart recovery, swipeable video galleries, voice-note feedback and some payment integrations may require paid plugins or custom development. The developer must confirm the implementation approach and cost before starting work.
